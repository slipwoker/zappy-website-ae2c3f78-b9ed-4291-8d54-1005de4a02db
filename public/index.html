Crafted with Intention

Considered Materials

We source recycled sterling silver, ethically mined gemstones, and conflict-free gold. Every element is chosen for its beauty and its story, ensuring your piece feels as good as it looks.

Made in Small Batches

Each collection is crafted in limited runs to reduce waste and celebrate individuality. We never mass-produce, so your piece is as unique as the hands that shaped it.

Packaging with Intention

From recycled paper boxes to organic cotton pouches, our packaging is designed to be kept, reused, or composted. No plastic, no excess — just a beautiful unboxing experience.

Repair-Minded Care

We believe jewellery should last a lifetime. We offer complimentary cleaning, restringing, and minor repairs on every Forge & Fleur piece, keeping your heirlooms in circulation.
